Commands & Tricks

Build communication. Strengthen reliability. Have fun together.

A strong training foundation starts with clear communication. The Commands & Tricks courses teach you how to introduce practical obedience behaviors, improve your dog's focus, and build reliability across everyday situations.

Whether you're teaching your dog their first commands or expanding an established vocabulary, these courses provide a structured approach to learning new behaviors through clear cues, consistent practice, and positive reinforcement.

Basic Commands & Tricks

Designed to establish a practical vocabulary of foundational behaviors, this course covers essential obedience skills alongside introductory tricks and body-awareness exercises.

You'll work on:

  • Foundational obedience: Sit, Down, Stay, and Free.
  • Recall and impulse control: Here, Leave It, and Focus.
  • Communication and guidance: Touch, Heel, and Off.
  • Everyday manners: Place and Back Up.
  • Introductory tricks: Paw, Roll Over, Spin, and Up.

As your dog progresses, you'll work toward more consistent responses, improved attention, and greater reliability in everyday situations.

Advanced Commands & Tricks

Ready to expand your dog's vocabulary? The advanced course introduces more complex movements, positions, and tricks that build on your existing training foundation.

You'll explore:

  • Positioning and movement: Orbit, Middle, Through, Chin, Play Dead, Fancy Paws, Dance, Jump, Under, and Over.
  • Advanced heelwork: Focus Heel
  • Object interaction: Get It and Release.

These lessons challenge you and your dog to refine communication, develop coordination, and learn more intricate behaviors through progressive practice.

Progress at Your Dog's Pace

Every dog learns differently. Work through the lessons consistently, practice in different environments, and gradually build reliability before increasing distractions or difficulty. Not every dog is safely capable of learning some of the more advanced tricks such as dance or jump, which heavily strain the hips and back legs of heavier dogs.

From everyday essentials to impressive tricks, build your dog's skills one command at a time.
