Pups for People

Independence grows when access is within reach.

Finding the right equipment, training, and reliable information can be difficult. We work to make those resources easier to find, and to help schools, businesses, and neighbors understand how to welcome service dog teams.

How we help

Practical support. More welcoming communities.

01

Service dog equipment

We connect disabled handlers with essential gear, including harnesses, leashes, and other equipment that supports everyday partnership.

02

Training opportunities

We help people find free training resources and connect eligible individuals with support for training that fits their needs.

03

Education and awareness

We help youth, businesses, and communities learn about service animals, respectful interactions, and ADA access.

Change starts together

Help make access and understanding part of everyday life.

Whether you give your time, make a donation, or share what you learn, you can help build a more supportive community for service dog handlers.
