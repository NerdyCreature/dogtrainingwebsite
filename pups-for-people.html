Our Goal

Help connect disabled people with service dog gear and free training courses across the USA, educate youth and businesses about ADA law, and normalize service animals in public spaces.

A world where every service dog team belongs.

100% of your donations go directly to service dog handlers and disabled individuals in need.

Clockwork Canine

Clockwork Canine helps dogs and their people build clear communication, confidence, and lasting skills. Our goal is to make training accessible for every stage of a dog's journey.
