Our Submission to The Congressional App Challenge

A look at the idea behind Clockwork Canine and our submission.

We’re excited to share our submission to The Congressional App Challenge: Clockwork Canine, an app concept built around a simple idea—dog owners should be able to find useful training education and professional support in one place.

Making training guidance easier to find

Training is part of life with every dog, but finding clear guidance for a particular stage or challenge can take time. Clockwork Canine brings together a library of courses that covers puppy foundations, basic commands, behavior challenges, and specialized areas such as service dog and competition training.

The goal is to make learning easier to return to. Owners can explore lessons at their own pace, revisit material when they need a refresher, and keep building skills as their dog grows and their goals change.

Connecting education with community

Our submission also highlights the value of learning with other people. Clockwork Canine is designed to help users connect with local trainers, ask questions, share progress, and learn from a community of dog owners and professionals.

That combination matters: practical information can help people get started, while access to knowledgeable trainers and a supportive community can help them decide what to do next.

Why we submitted

We submitted Clockwork Canine because we believe technology can make dog training education more approachable and easier to access. The app brings courses, trainer connections, and community features together around the everyday experience of caring for and training a dog.

Thank you for taking the time to read about our submission and the idea behind it. We’ll continue working toward a resource that helps dogs and their people learn together.
