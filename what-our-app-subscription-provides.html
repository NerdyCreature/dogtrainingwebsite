What Our App Subscription Provides

More than training. A community built around you and your dog.

ClockWork Canine brings dog training education, professional guidance, and a community of fellow dog owners together in one place. Your subscription unlocks the full experience, giving you access to our entire course library and additional features designed to help you learn, connect, and share your journey.

Whether you're raising your first puppy, working through a training challenge, or looking to connect with experienced handlers, ClockWork Canine gives you more ways to grow alongside your dog.

What's included?

Everything you need to learn, connect, and grow.

  1. Complete Course Library

    Every course. One subscription.

    Gain access to our entire collection of dog training courses, covering everything from puppy foundations and basic obedience to specialized training topics and competitions. Explore educational resources, revisit lessons whenever you need a refresher, and discover new ways to support your dog's development.

  2. Connect With Local Trainers

    Professional advice, made easy.

    Unlock the ability to contact and communicate with local trainers through the app. Connect with professionals in your area to discuss training goals, seek guidance, and explore in-person opportunities for additional support.

  3. Subscriber Profile Icon

    Show the community you're a subscriber.

    Unlock an exclusive subscriber icon displayed on your social profile, giving your profile a distinctive touch within the ClockWork Canine community.

  4. Daily Post Boost

    Give your posts a little extra reach.

    Boost one post per day to increase its visibility within the app's social community. Whether you're sharing a training milestone, asking a question, or celebrating your dog's progress, a boost helps give your post an additional opportunity to be seen.

  5. Expanded Profile Customization

    Make your profile your own.

    Unlock additional profile customization options to personalize your space in the ClockWork Canine community. Show off your dogs, express your interests, and create a profile that reflects you and your training journey.
